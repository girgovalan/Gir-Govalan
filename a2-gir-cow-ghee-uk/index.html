Is bilona ghee different from the jars in Tesco?

Often yes. Ours is curd-churned from Gir cow milk. Many retail jars follow a faster cream route. Smell and cooking aroma are the easy home test.

Does cold UK weather ruin ghee?

No. It may firm up. That is texture, not spoilage. Keep the lid tight and the spoon dry.
