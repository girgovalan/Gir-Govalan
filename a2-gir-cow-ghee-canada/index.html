A note on climate and storage

In January the ghee may firm up. In July it may look softer. Both are normal for pure ghee. Trouble starts when water from a wet spoon gets in, or when the jar sits open next to the stove. Keep it closed, dry, and away from direct heat. No fridge needed unless your kitchen runs very warm.

What makes our jar different

Big-box labels can look traditional and still be cream-based or blended. Ask for breed, process, and a contact number. If the seller only answers with stock photos, keep searching.

Major cities and many suburbs. Confirm your postal code on WhatsApp before paying.

My ghee hardened in winter — is it bad?

No. Pure ghee firms in cold rooms. Warm the spoonful gently; the jar itself is fine if it smells clean.

Can I use it for Punjabi and Gujarati cooking?

Yes. It is everyday desi ghee — tadka, roti, sweets, all of it.

What size for a family of five?

If you cook Indian food most days, 1 litre usually lasts longer between orders. Light users can stay at 500 ml.
