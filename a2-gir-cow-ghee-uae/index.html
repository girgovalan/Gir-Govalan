Will the ghee melt or spoil in summer heat?

Pure ghee softens in heat — that is normal. Spoilage usually comes from wet spoons or leaving the lid open. Keep it sealed and dry.

Is this okay for everyday Emirati-Indian meals?

Yes. Use it anywhere you would use desi ghee at home.
